ArcadeBoard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

ArcadeBoard is a soundboard app. It is built to work entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. ArcadeBoard does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no servers.

What stays on your phone

  • The sounds, pictures and boards you create are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Photos you choose for pads are copied (downscaled) into the app; the app only sees the photos you pick.
  • Audio you share into ArcadeBoard (for example a WhatsApp voice note) is edited on your device, including trimming, recording over a part and effects.

Sharing

When you send a sound or export a board or backup, ArcadeBoard hands a file to your phone's share sheet. Where it goes next (WhatsApp, Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive…) is your choice and is governed by that app's privacy policy. ArcadeBoard itself never uploads anything.

Purchases

ArcadeBoard Unlimited is a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their privacy policies. ArcadeBoard never sees your payment details or your Apple ID / Google account. The app only asks the store whether Unlimited has been bought on your account, so it can turn it on (and restore it on a new phone).

Permissions

  • Microphone — only while you record your own voice over part of a clip. The recording stays on your phone.
  • Photos — only when you choose a picture for a pad.
  • No camera, location or contacts permissions are used.

Children

ArcadeBoard does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
