What it does

A tiny sampler for the group chat.

01

Save the best bits

Share any voice note into ArcadeBoard straight from your chat app. Zoom into the waveform and pull out the perfect three seconds.

02

Edit like a pro

Record your own voice over any part, insert a new bit, or cut a piece out of the middle. Everything is undoable.

03

19 effects, stacked

Echo, reverb, chipmunk, deep voice, slow-mo, robot, walkie-talkie, 8-bit, stutter… put them on any part and stack them.

04

Pads that feel real

Every sound gets a pad with its own LED and mini waveform. Add a friend’s photo, sort pads into folders, drag them around.

05

Fire it back

Flip to Send mode and one tap sends the clip into any chat — as a voice note.

06

Share whole boards

Export a board as one file and drop it in the group. Friends tap it to import; boards merge when someone adds sounds.
