ArcadeBoard — Support

ArcadeBoard is a soundboard for voice notes: save the best bits from your group chat, trim and edit them, add effects, and send them back as voice notes.

Contact

Email natanaelsilva@gmail.com. Please include your phone model and what you were doing.

Getting sounds in

  • In WhatsApp (or Files, Voice Memos, Telegram…): long-press a voice note → Share → ArcadeBoard Clip. The clipper opens; trim, edit, add effects and save.
  • On a board: tap + → Audio from Files.
  • A friend's board: open the .arcadeboard file they sent and import it.

Sending a sound back

Flip the switch on a board to Send, tap a pad and pick the chat. It arrives as a voice note.

ArcadeBoard Unlimited

  • The first 20 sounds you make are free. Boards friends share and the Starter Pack never count.
  • Unlimited is a one-time purchase. On a new phone, open Settings → ArcadeBoard Unlimited → Restore purchase (same Apple ID / Google account).
  • Refunds go through Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play.

Backups

Settings → Back up everything makes one file with all your boards. Keep it in Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive, and use Restore from a backup on a new phone.

Privacy

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone. Privacy policy.

Open source licences

ArcadeBoard uses FFmpeg under the LGPL. To relink the app against a modified FFmpeg, you can get its object files free of charge by email (see Settings → Open source licenses in the app).
